## 5. Summary and Recommendations

### Critical (blocks correct UX in non-English locales)
1. **Terms & Privacy pages are completely untranslated** in both fr and ar (70 keys). These are legal pages -- they should either be translated or hidden in non-English locales.
2. **Breeding module is entirely hardcoded** -- ~34 strings across 5 files never use `useTranslations`. Need to add `breeding.*` keys to all 3 locales and wrap strings in `t()`.
3. **auth.signup terms agreement** (5 keys) missing in both fr and ar -- users signing up in French/Arabic will see broken text.

### High (user-visible English in non-English locales)
4. **136 untranslated placeholders in fr.json** -- many are common words like "patients", "date", "notes" that have obvious French equivalents.
5. **126 untranslated placeholders in ar.json** -- includes entire team management and health alerts sections.
6. **Developers page** fully untranslated in both fr and ar (37 keys each).

### Low
7. **5 orphan keys** in fr.json and 2 in ar.json not present in en.json -- should be added to en or removed.
8. **sidebar.tsx** hardcoded strings are shadcn boilerplate (cosmetic, screen-reader only).
9. **Header brand name "Vetara"** -- likely intentional, confirm with PO.
