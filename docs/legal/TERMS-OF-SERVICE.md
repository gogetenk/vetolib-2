## 1. Description of the Service

Vetolib is a cloud-based veterinary clinic management platform ("SaaS") that provides:

- **Appointment scheduling** — calendar management, online booking, reminders
- **Patient records** — animal medical records, consultation notes, prescriptions
- **Client management** — pet owner contact information and communication
- **Billing and invoicing** — invoice generation, payment tracking
- **Messaging** — clinic-to-client communication (WhatsApp, SMS, email)
- **AI-assisted features** — scheduling optimization, triage suggestions, no-show prediction (where available)

The Service is designed for licensed veterinary clinics and their authorized staff. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional veterinary judgment.
