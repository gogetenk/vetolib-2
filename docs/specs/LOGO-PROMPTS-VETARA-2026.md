# Logo Prompts — Vetara (2026)

> 3 prompts for Nano Banana image generation.
> Product: Vetara — AI-powered veterinary clinic management SaaS (UAE + France markets).

---

## Prompt 1 — Minimalist / Abstract Icon (Favicon + App Header)

```
A single minimalist logomark icon for a modern veterinary SaaS called "Vetara". No text, icon only. The shape is a clean geometric abstract mark that subtly evokes both animal care and technology — think intersecting arcs forming a stylized protective shield around an abstract animal silhouette, or a geometric leaf-heart hybrid suggesting health and life. Do NOT use a paw print, stethoscope, or cross. Style: flat vector, sharp geometric edges with one subtle curve for warmth. Color: deep teal-emerald gradient (#0D9488 to #065F46) on a fully transparent background. The icon must read clearly at 16x16 favicon size and at 64x64. Square canvas, centered composition, generous negative space. Professional, trustworthy, premium SaaS aesthetic. No shadows, no 3D effects, no gradients beyond the single teal-to-emerald sweep.
```

---

## Prompt 2 — Logo with "Vetara" Text (Landing Page + Marketing)

```
A horizontal logo lockup for a modern veterinary SaaS. On the left: the geometric teal-emerald abstract icon from Prompt 1 — a minimalist mark evoking animal care and technology (no paw print, no stethoscope). On the right: the word "Vetara" in a clean modern sans-serif typeface (similar to Inter or Satoshi), medium weight, letter-spacing slightly wide. The text color is dark charcoal (#1C1917) for contrast. The icon uses a deep teal-emerald palette (#0D9488 to #065F46). Transparent background. The icon and text are vertically centered with comfortable spacing between them. The overall feel is premium, trustworthy, and tech-forward — like a modern healthcare or fintech brand. Flat vector style, no shadows, no decorative elements. Horizontal format with roughly 3:1 width-to-height ratio.
```

## Prompt 3 — Pixel Art 16-bit SNES Sprite (GitHub Forge / Fun Branding)

```
A 128x128 pixel art sprite in 16-bit SNES style, inspired by Final Fantasy VI and Chrono Trigger. The scene shows a small veterinary forge workshop: a glowing anvil in the center with bright orange-yellow sparks flying upward, surrounded by 3-4 tiny pixel-art robots (2-head-tall chibi style) busily working — one hammering, one carrying a potion bottle shaped like a pet medicine vial, one reading a tiny scroll. The forge has a warm campfire glow at the base. A small pixel banner or sign above reads "VETARA" in a chunky retro pixel font. Color palette: warm oranges and ambers (#F59E0B, #EA580C, #DC2626) for the forge and sparks against a dark navy-charcoal background (#1E1B2E). The robots have teal accents (#0D9488) matching the brand. Visible individual pixels, limited color palette (max 32 colors), nostalgic retro RPG aesthetic. No anti-aliasing, crisp pixel edges.
```
