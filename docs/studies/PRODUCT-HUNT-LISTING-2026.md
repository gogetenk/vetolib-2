## 3. Full Description

### What is Vetolib?

Vetolib is a full-stack veterinary practice management system designed from scratch for modern clinics. It replaces the patchwork of legacy tools, spreadsheets, and WhatsApp groups that most vet practices still rely on.

From online booking to AI-powered triage, from medical records to invoicing — Vetolib handles the entire clinic workflow in a single, unified platform.

### Why we built it

Veterinary clinics are stuck in 2010. Most practice management software was designed before smartphones existed. The tools are clunky, expensive, disconnected, and painful to use. Meanwhile, veterinarians are burning out — not because of the medicine, but because of the admin.

We asked a simple question: what if a vet clinic could run as smoothly as a modern tech company? What if scheduling, records, billing, and communication just worked — without 6 different tools and a drawer full of paper?

### How it works

- **Smart Scheduling** — Calendar views (day/week/month) with conflict detection, color-coded consultation types, and Ramadan-aware operating hours
- **Online Booking Portal** — Pet owners book appointments directly. No phone tag. Configurable availability per vet, per service
- **Medical Records** — Full patient history, prescriptions, treatments, vaccination tracking. Everything linked to the pet and the owner
- **AI Triage** — When a pet owner describes symptoms, Vetolib suggests urgency level and recommends booking as routine or emergency. Clear AI disclaimer, always transparent
- **No-Show Prediction** — ML-powered scoring to help clinics optimize their schedule. Internal only — never shown to pet owners
- **Invoicing & Billing** — Generate invoices, track payments, apply VAT. Designed for UAE tax compliance (AED currency, 5% VAT)
- **Multi-Tenant by Design** — Each clinic is fully isolated. Data never leaks between practices
- **Multilingual** — English and Arabic (RTL) out of the box. French and Polish coming soon

### Who is it for?

- Independent vet clinics looking to modernize
- Multi-location veterinary groups needing a unified platform
- Vet practices in the UAE, France, and Poland (our launch markets)
- Any clinic tired of juggling 5+ tools to manage their day

### Tech under the hood

Built on .NET Aspire, ASP.NET Core 10, Next.js 15, PostgreSQL. Modular monolith architecture — each feature is an isolated module that can evolve independently. BDD-tested with 680+ automated tests. Not a weekend project — this is production-grade infrastructure.

## 7. First Day Strategy — Launch Day Checklist

### Timing
- [ ] Launch at **12:01 AM PST** (Product Hunt day resets at midnight Pacific)
- [ ] Best days: **Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday** — avoid Monday (high competition) and Friday (low traffic)

### Pre-Launch (2-4 weeks before)
- [ ] Build a "coming soon" page with email capture (target: 200+ signups)
- [ ] Engage on Product Hunt daily for 30+ days: upvote, comment thoughtfully on other products, build profile credibility
- [ ] Recruit a **hunter** with a strong following (ideally 1000+ followers) — reach out personally, not via cold DM
- [ ] Prepare all gallery assets, thumbnail (240x240), and video
- [ ] Write and refine the maker comment (see section 5)
- [ ] Draft 10+ social media posts (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp groups) scheduled throughout launch day
- [ ] Prepare a launch blog post with the full story (SEO + credibility)
- [ ] Line up 3-5 friendly beta users who can leave genuine, detailed reviews on launch day

### Launch Day (T+0)
- [ ] Post the maker comment within 2 minutes of going live
- [ ] Send personal messages to warm contacts: "We just launched on Product Hunt, would love your honest feedback" — NOT "please upvote"
- [ ] Share on Twitter/X with a personal thread (the story, not just "we launched")
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn with the founder story angle
- [ ] Share in relevant communities: vet tech groups, SaaS founder communities, UAE tech ecosystem, indie hacker forums
- [ ] **Respond to EVERY comment within 30 minutes** — all day. This is non-negotiable.
- [ ] Post progress updates throughout the day ("We just hit 100 upvotes — here's a feature we haven't talked about yet...")
- [ ] Monitor and engage from 9 AM to 11 PM PST minimum

### Post-Launch (Day 2-7)
- [ ] Thank everyone who commented or upvoted (personal DMs to top commenters)
- [ ] Write a "lessons learned" post about the launch
- [ ] Follow up with every lead that signed up during launch day
- [ ] If you win Product of the Day/Week, update your website with the badge immediately

## 8. Upvote Strategy — Ethical, No Bots

### The Rules
- **Never ask for upvotes directly.** Ask people to "check out our launch" or "share your feedback." Product Hunt's algorithm penalizes coordinated voting.
- **Never use bots, upvote services, or paid upvotes.** Products get unfeatured and banned. Not worth it.
- **New accounts carry less weight.** Votes from active Product Hunt users with history matter 10x more than fresh accounts.

### Building Authentic Support

**Tier 1 — Inner Circle (50-100 people)**
- Friends, family, colleagues who already use Product Hunt or will create accounts 2+ weeks early
- Beta users and early adopters who genuinely love the product
- Fellow founders from your network — offer to support their launches in return
- Action: Personal message (not mass email), explain why their support matters

**Tier 2 — Professional Network (200-500 people)**
- Veterinary communities: vet school alumni groups, vet tech Facebook groups, veterinary subreddits
- UAE tech ecosystem: Dubai startup groups, Abu Dhabi tech meetups, MENA founder communities
- SaaS communities: Indie Hackers, SaaS Twitter, MicroConf, r/SaaS
- Action: Share the story, not just the link. "We built this for vets who are drowning in admin — would love your honest take"

**Tier 3 — Public Amplification**
- Twitter/X thread telling the founder story (aim for 10+ tweets, personal, raw)
- LinkedIn post: "Why I left my job to build software for veterinarians" angle
- Email newsletter to your waiting list (if you built one pre-launch)
- Cross-post on Hacker News (Show HN) — different audience, complementary reach

### What Actually Moves the Needle
1. **Comments > Upvotes.** A product with 200 upvotes and 80 comments will rank higher than one with 400 upvotes and 10 comments. Encourage questions and discussion.
2. **Engagement velocity matters.** Consistent upvotes throughout the day beat a morning spike that dies off.
3. **The maker's responsiveness signals quality.** If people see the founder answering every question thoughtfully, they upvote out of respect.
4. **Genuine reviews from real users are gold.** A beta user saying "I've been using this for 3 months and it cut my admin time in half" is worth more than 50 anonymous upvotes.
