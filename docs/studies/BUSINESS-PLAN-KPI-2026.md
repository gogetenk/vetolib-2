## 2. Sales Activities

### Kinga -- UAE Market (20h/week)

Kinga is based in Dubai and handles the primary market (UAE).

**Weekly activity targets:**
| Activity | Volume | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp outreach | 50 messages/week | Direct to clinic managers, personalized |
| Email outreach | 25 emails/week | Cold + warm follow-ups |
| Demos | 3 demos/week | Zoom/Google Meet, 30-min format |
| Clinic visits | 1-2/week | In-person when possible (Dubai, Abu Dhabi) |
| Follow-ups | As needed | WhatsApp preferred in UAE |

**UAE outreach strategy:**
- WhatsApp is the primary business communication channel in UAE -- email open rates are low
- Personalized messages referencing the clinic's Google reviews, services, or recent posts
- Arabic + English bilingual messaging
- Demo scheduling via WhatsApp, not calendar links (cultural preference for personal interaction)
- Focus on Dubai first, then Abu Dhabi, then Sharjah/Ajman

**Monthly targets for Kinga:**
- 200+ WhatsApp contacts reached
- 100+ emails sent
- 12+ demos delivered
- 3-4 trial signups
- 1-2 conversions to paid

### Yannis -- France Market (10h/week)

**Weekly activity targets:**
| Activity | Volume | Channel |

## 3. Financial Model

### Monthly Burn Rate

| Category | Monthly Cost (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud infrastructure (Azure/Aspire) | $30-50 | Minimal at early scale |
| Domain + SaaS tools | $20-30 | Email, analytics, CRM |
| AI API costs (Claude, etc.) | $50-80 | Development + product features |
| Marketing (ads, content) | $30-50 | Mostly organic at start |
| Miscellaneous | $20-30 | Contingency |
| **Total burn** | **$160-200/month** | |

**Key insight:** Burn is extremely low because:
- No office (fully remote)
- No salaries (founders only, no external hires until $5K MRR)
- AI-assisted development dramatically reduces external dev costs
- Infrastructure is pay-as-you-go with minimal base cost

### Break-Even Analysis

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Monthly burn | ~$180 (midpoint) |
| Average revenue per client | ~$49/month (blended Starter) |
| **Break-even point** | **~4-5 paying clients** |
| Target break-even date | **June 2026** (end of Q2) |

### Runway

With near-zero burn, runway is effectively unlimited (funded by founders' other income). This removes the typical startup pressure to raise capital early and allows for deliberate, quality-focused growth.
