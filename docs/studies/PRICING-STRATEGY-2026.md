# Pricing Strategy -- Vetolib / Vetara (2026)

> Per-vet pricing model with feature tiers, calibrated for UAE launch and France expansion.

---

## 1. Pricing Model: Per-Vet with Feature Tiers

### Why Per-Vet?

- **Predictable for clinics** -- Costs scale linearly with headcount, easy to budget
- **Aligned with value** -- Each vet added generates revenue for the clinic; our pricing grows with their growth
- **Industry standard** -- ezyVet, Provet, and most competitors use per-vet or per-user pricing
- **Simple to communicate** -- No complex usage-based metering, no surprise bills

### Why NOT Per-Clinic Flat Rate?

- A 1-vet clinic and a 10-vet clinic have vastly different needs and revenue potential
- Flat rate either overcharges small clinics or undercharges large ones
- Per-vet ensures fair pricing across all clinic sizes

### Why NOT Free Tier?

- Free tiers attract non-converting users who consume support resources
- Veterinary software is a professional tool -- professionals expect to pay for professional tools
- A 30-day free trial provides enough time to evaluate without creating a permanent free-rider base
- Break-even is at 5 paying clients -- a free tier delays this unnecessarily

## 4. Trial & Conversion Strategy

### 30-Day Free Trial

- Full Pro-tier access during trial (show them the best experience)
- No credit card required to start (reduce friction)
- Credit card required at day 14 to continue (qualify serious prospects)
- Automated email sequence during trial:
  - Day 1: Welcome + setup guide
  - Day 7: Feature highlights they haven't tried
  - Day 14: Credit card prompt + case study
  - Day 21: "7 days left" + ROI calculator
  - Day 28: Final push + offer to extend if engaged

### No Free Tier

A deliberate strategic choice. Reasons:
1. Support costs for free users erode margin before we have scale
2. Free users rarely convert in B2B SaaS (< 5% typical)
3. Professional tools should communicate professional value
4. Our break-even is 5 paying clients -- every free account delays this

## 5. Target Segment

### Primary: Clinics with 3-5 Veterinarians

This is the sweet spot because:
- **Large enough** to feel pain from manual processes (scheduling conflicts, lost records, billing errors)
- **Small enough** that they cannot afford dedicated IT staff or enterprise solutions
- **Growing** -- they are actively hiring, adding services, and need scalable tools
- **Decision-making** is fast -- typically 1-2 decision-makers (clinic owner + practice manager)
