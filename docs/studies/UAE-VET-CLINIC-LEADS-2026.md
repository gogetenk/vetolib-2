## Key Observations for Vetolib Sales Strategy

1. **Dubai dominates** — 42 clinics found (60% of total). Highest density in Jumeirah, Al Barsha, JLT, DSO, and Motor City.
2. **Multi-branch chains are prime targets** — Modern Vet (7+ branches), The City Vet (9 branches), Noble Vet (3 branches), and Pawsitive (2 branches) would benefit most from a centralized booking/management platform.
3. **24/7 emergency clinics** need robust scheduling and on-call management — key Vetolib differentiator.
4. **Specialist niches exist** — The Cat Vet (cats only), Abu Dhabi Falcon Hospital (falcons), Avian Vet (falcons), 2Feet4Paws (exotic). These need customizable consultation types.
5. **Northern Emirates are underserved** — Ajman (1 clinic), RAK (2), UAQ (1), Fujairah (0 found). Lower competition but smaller market.
6. **Many clinics lack online booking** — majority rely on phone/WhatsApp. Strong opportunity for Vetolib.
7. **International branding is common** — British, European, American, German, Canadian, Australian, Vienna. Clinics cater to expat communities.
