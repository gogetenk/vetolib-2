> Deep Dive: How Vetolib's AI SOAP Notes actually work.
>
> SOAP (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) notes are the backbone of veterinary medical records. They're also one of the biggest time sinks in clinical practice.
>
> Here's what happens in Vetolib:
>
> 1. The vet completes a consultation as normal
> 2. Key observations are entered via quick-select fields (symptoms, vitals, findings)
> 3. Our AI engine generates a complete, structured SOAP note in seconds
> 4. The vet reviews, edits if needed, and approves
>
> What makes this different from generic AI transcription:
>
> -- Trained on veterinary terminology (not human medicine)
> -- Species-aware (a cat's normal heart rate is not a dog's)
> -- Follows AAHA documentation standards
> -- The vet ALWAYS has final approval -- AI assists, never replaces
>
> Average time saved per consultation: 8 minutes. Over a full day, that's an extra hour of clinical time.
