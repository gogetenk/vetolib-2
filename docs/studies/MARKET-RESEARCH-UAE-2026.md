## 1. Executive Summary

The UAE veterinary software market is small but fast-growing: USD 4.6M in 2024, projected USD 10.0M by 2030 (CAGR 14.1%). Pet ownership has exploded — from 588,700 pets in 2014 to over 2 million in 2025 — driven by expat lifestyles, millennial "fur baby" culture, and stricter animal welfare laws. Dubai alone has 70+ veterinary clinics and 500+ practicing veterinarians.

Despite this growth, no dominant veterinary practice management software (VPMS) is purpose-built for the UAE market. Clinics either use global platforms (ezyVet, Digitail, Provet Cloud) that lack Arabic/RTL support, or regional solutions (vetPMS, MEDAS) that lack modern UX and AI features. This creates a clear gap for Vetolib.

**Key takeaway:** A cloud-native, bilingual (EN+AR), AI-powered VPMS with UAE-specific features (falcon/camel species, BNPL payments, Ramadan scheduling) has no direct competitor today.

## 6. Pain Points & Unmet Needs

### 6.1 Pain Points Reported by Veterinarians

Based on industry reports, reviews, and forums:

1. **Documentation burden:** Veterinarians spend 1+ hours/day on SOAP notes and clinical documentation. Most VPMS still require manual data entry. AI scribes are emerging but penetration is only 10-25% of practices.

2. **Poor mobile experience:** Mobile access remains "surprisingly limited" across most platforms, despite vets needing field access (equine, farm calls, emergencies).

3. **Slow onboarding:** Implementation takes 1-3 months for most enterprise systems. Small clinics need faster setup.

4. **Integration fragmentation:** Practices use 5-8 separate tools (PMS, labs, imaging, payments, communication, booking). Most don't integrate well, causing "manual work or jumping between systems."

5. **Inventory & prescription issues:** Even modern platforms like Digitail have reported issues with inventory and prescription management.

6. **Support responsiveness:** Multiple platforms receive complaints about slow customer support response times.

7. **Pricing opacity:** Most vendors hide pricing behind "contact sales" — frustrating for small clinic owners comparing options.

### 6.2 Gaps Not Covered by ANY Competitor

These represent clear opportunities for differentiation:

| Gap | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Arabic/RTL native interface** | Zero major VPMS offers a proper Arabic RTL interface. UAE has a significant Arabic-speaking clientele. |
| **Camel & falcon species support** | UAE has the world's largest falcon hospital (11,000 patients/year) and the world's first camel hospital. No VPMS has species-specific templates for these animals. |
| **BNPL integration (Tabby/Tamara)** | Buy Now Pay Later is dominant in UAE retail (46% mobile wallet adoption). No VPMS integrates Tabby or Tamara for veterinary payments. |
| **Ramadan-aware scheduling** | UAE clinics adjust hours during Ramadan. No VPMS has built-in Ramadan scheduling support. |
| **UAE regulatory compliance** | Federal Law No. 9/2017, animal welfare regulations, MOCCAE licensing requirements — no VPMS has built-in compliance features. |
| **Multi-currency GCC** | Clinics in GCC operate in AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, BHD, OMR. No single VPMS handles all GCC currencies natively. |
| **Sunday-Thursday work week** | UAE standard work week is Sun-Thu. Most VPMS default to Mon-Fri scheduling. |

## 7. UAE Specificities

### 7.1 Regulatory Environment

| Regulation | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Governing body** | Ministry of Climate Change and Environment (MOCCAE) |
| **Veterinary practice law** | Federal Law No. 10/2002 on Practicing the Veterinary Medicine Profession |
| **Animal welfare** | Federal Law No. 16/2007 on Animal Welfare |
| **Facility regulation** | Ministerial Resolution No. 384/2020 on Regulation of Veterinary Facilities |
| **Pharma products** | Federal Decree-Law (2025) on Veterinary Medical Products — licensing via Emirates Drug Establishment + MOCCAE |
| **Licensing** | All veterinarians must be licensed by and registered with MOCCAE; facility licenses renewed every 5 years |
| **Drug tracking** | Veterinary pharmaceutical establishments require MOCCAE license before any activity |

**Software implication:** A VPMS serving UAE clinics should support MOCCAE-compliant record keeping, drug dispensing logs, and license tracking.

### 7.2 Species-Specific Requirements

The UAE has unique veterinary needs not found in Western markets:

| Species | Context | Software Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Falcons** | Abu Dhabi Falcon Hospital treats ~11,000 falcons/year. Falconry is a core cultural practice. | Avian-specific templates, microchip (PIT) tracking, feather imping records, endoscopy records, quarantine management |
| **Camels** | Dubai Camel Hospital (world's first, $11M facility, 65 staff). Racing camels are high-value assets. | Large animal templates, racing injury protocols, rehabilitation tracking (mini-racetrack), anti-doping compliance, breeding/reproduction records |
| **Horses** | Abu Dhabi Equine & Camel Hospital (200,000 sqm). Active racing industry. | Equine-specific records, racing fitness tracking, syndicate ownership, performance history |
| **Exotic pets** | Growing trend in UAE (reptiles, birds, small mammals) | Multi-species template system, species-specific drug dosing |

### 7.3 Language & Localization

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Primary UI language** | English (business language of UAE) |
| **Secondary UI language** | Arabic (required for Emirati clients and government-facing documents) |
| **RTL support** | Mandatory for Arabic interface |
| **Client communication** | Bilingual (EN+AR) SMS, emails, discharge summaries |
| **Currency** | AED (UAE Dirham) |
| **Timezone** | Asia/Dubai (UTC+4, no DST) |
| **Date format** | DD/MM/YYYY (common in UAE) |
| **Work week** | Sunday to Thursday (configurable per clinic) |
| **Ramadan** | Reduced operating hours; configurable scheduling |

### 7.4 Demographic Context

- UAE population: ~10M (88% expatriates)
- High disposable income; willingness to pay for premium pet care
- Millennial and Gen-Z "fur baby" culture driving pet ownership growth
- 1.5M+ pet owners, 2M+ pets (cats outnumber dogs 2:1)
- Dubai and Abu Dhabi are the primary markets (highest concentration of affluent pet owners)

### 8.3 BNPL Opportunity

Buy Now Pay Later is a significant opportunity for veterinary clinics in the UAE:
- Vet bills for surgery/emergency can exceed AED 5,000-10,000
- Tabby and Tamara are already being adopted by healthcare/medical clinics
- Minimum spend for payment links: AED 2,500
- Tabby card holders can pay up to AED 10,000/day
- No VPMS currently integrates BNPL — this is a clear first-mover advantage for Vetolib
