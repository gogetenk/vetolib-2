## Sending Cadence — Recommended Sequence

| Day | Channel | Template |
|---|---|---|
| D0 | Email | Cold Email #1 — Introduction |
| D0 | LinkedIn | InMail (if decision-maker profile found) |
| D+2 | WhatsApp | Introduction (if phone number available) |
| D+3 | Email | Cold Email #2 — Follow-up |
| D+5 | WhatsApp | Demo Invite (if any engagement) |
| D+7 | Email | Cold Email #3 — Last Chance |
| Post-demo | Email | After Demo (within 2 hours of demo) |

**Rules:**
- Never send more than 1 message per channel per day
- WhatsApp only during UAE business hours (Sun-Thu, 9am-6pm GST)
- If they reply "not interested" on any channel, stop all outreach immediately
- Multi-branch chains get Template #7 instead of #1 as first touch
- Log all interactions in CRM before sending next touchpoint

## Personalization Checklist

Before sending any template, verify:

- [ ] `{clinic_name}` matches their exact branding (e.g., "Modern Vet" not "Modern Veterinary")
- [ ] `{owner_name}` is the actual decision-maker (not the receptionist)
- [ ] `{city}` is correct (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Sharjah, etc.)
- [ ] `{branch_count}` is accurate for multi-branch templates
- [ ] Opening line references something specific about their clinic (services, history, specialty)
- [ ] Arabic version reviewed by native speaker before sending
- [ ] Phone number verified before WhatsApp outreach
